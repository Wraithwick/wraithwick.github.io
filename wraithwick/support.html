How do I get Season 1 back on a new phone?

Install Wraithwick, sign in to the store with the same account you bought with (the same Google account on Android, the same Apple ID on iPhone and iPad), then open Settings → Restore purchases (the Season 1 screen has the same button). Season 1 is a one-time purchase that belongs to your store account, so it is free to restore as often as you need.

Purchases cannot move between Android and iOS: Google and Apple keep separate records. Family Sharing is not supported; the purchase belongs to one account.

I bought Season 1 but districts are still locked

  • Connect to the internet and tap Restore purchases in Settings.
  • Check that the store app is signed in to the account you bought with.
  • Some payment methods (for example cash or bank transfer in some countries) leave the purchase pending. The game unlocks Season 1 as soon as the store confirms the payment.
  • Still locked? Email me the date of purchase and the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt (never your card details).

My progress is gone. Can I get it back?

Wraithwick has no accounts and no cloud save: your progress (solved cases, notes, seals, daily streak, your detective) is stored only on your device. I cannot see it or restore it from my side.

  • Android: if Google backup is on for your device, Android saves the game's progress and settings to your Google account and restores them when you reinstall or move to a new phone with the same account (including phone-to-phone transfer). Ad and privacy choices are not copied; the game asks again.
  • iPhone and iPad: an iCloud or computer backup of the device includes the game's data and brings it back when you restore that backup.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its data without such a backup deletes the progress for good.
  • Purchases are never lost: restore them as described above.

How do I stop ads or change my ad choices?

Season 1 and No Ads remove interstitial ads between cases. Optional rewarded videos remain available only when you choose to watch one. Without either purchase, an interstitial may be offered after every three eligible completed cases, only when you continue from a result screen; tutorial cases do not count. If UMP provides a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen it in Settings → Ad privacy. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by the store that processed the payment, under its current rules. Use Google Play's purchase history or Apple's report-a-problem page. We cannot promise that a request will be approved. Details and links are on the Refunds page.

How do I report a bug?

Email wraithwick.studio@gmail.com with:

  • what happened and what you expected, and the steps that lead to it;
  • your device model and system version (for example "Pixel 7, Android 15");
  • the game's version: on Android, Settings → Apps → Wraithwick (the version is at the bottom); on iPhone, Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Wraithwick;
  • a screenshot if you can, and the case name if it happened in a case.

If you think a case is unfair or has two answers, tell me which case it was. Every case is checked by a solver to have exactly one answer reachable by logic, so a report like that is very valuable.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings. Data held by Google for ads (and, if enabled, for analytics) is explained in the Privacy Policy, together with how to ask me to delete it.
