Refunds

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Purchases in the current Android release are processed by Google Play. If an iOS version is distributed later, Apple's App Store will process purchases for that version. To request a refund, use the purchase history or support process provided by the store that processed your payment. The store reviews requests under its current policies; we cannot promise that a request will be approved or set a separate refund period here.

Google Play

Open Google Play order history, select the purchase and follow the available refund or problem-reporting steps. See Google's current refund request instructions and refund policy. For a purchase or access problem, contact us at wraithwick.studio@gmail.com with the order reference; never send payment-card details.

Apple App Store (if an iOS version is distributed)

Request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple reviews App Store refund requests under its policies. See Apple's refund request instructions.

Access after a refund

If the store reports that a purchase is no longer owned, the associated paid access may be locked the next time the game refreshes store entitlements. Local game progress is not intentionally removed by that change. Restore purchases in the game using the same store account if an eligible purchase is missing.

Consumer rights

This page does not limit mandatory rights or remedies you may have under consumer-protection law. If you have a question about a purchase, contact the store or write to wraithwick.studio@gmail.com.
