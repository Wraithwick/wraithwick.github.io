Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

Wraithwick is published by Pavlo Tkachov under the Wraithwick Studio brand ("we"). Wraithwick Studio is a brand name, not a separate legal entity. Contact us at wraithwick.studio@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, case notes, your detective profile (including a name you enter), settings, reminder preferences, consent choices, purchase-entitlement cache and ad counters on your device. There are no player accounts or game-progress servers. A daily reminder is scheduled locally after you enable it. Operating-system backups may copy app data according to your device settings. A result card is created locally and shared only when you choose an app in the system share sheet.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases in the current Android release. The game checks the Google Play account's product entitlements to unlock or restore purchases; Google handles payment details. If an iOS version is distributed later, Apple's App Store will process purchases for that version under its own terms. Season 1 and No Ads are one-time purchases. Season 1 unlocks the current Season 1 content and full case archive. Both purchases suppress interstitial ads. Neither removes optional rewarded videos, which remain available only when you choose to watch one. Purchases do not create a Wraithwick account or send purchase data to a Wraithwick server.

Analytics and Remote Config

Wraithwick uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which cases are started, completed or abandoned and how hints and mistakes affect play. Events use case identifiers, counts and short status values; the game does not send the detective name you enter, free-form text, clue text or individual board moves. Firebase may also process an app-instance identifier, device and app information, language, approximate region and session information.

Analytics events also cover how game features are used, such as the daily case, streaks, reminders and achievements, as well as purchases and ads: opening purchase screens and offers; tapping locked content; tapping Buy or Restore; whether a purchase or restore succeeded, failed or was cancelled; and whether an ad was offered, shown, completed, skipped or failed. These events can include a product identifier, the screen or offer where the action happened, and a short result or reason. Google Analytics may also record purchase events automatically, including the product identifier, name and price. These events do not include payment-card details.

Firebase Remote Config delivers app settings such as feature switches and gameplay limits. It uses technical app-instance, device, app-version, language and region information to return the appropriate configuration. Analytics collection is consent-gated where the app asks for consent, and can be changed in Settings → Privacy choices. Outside the regions where an opt-in is requested, analytics is enabled by default and can be turned off there.

Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics sends crash and serious-error reports to help diagnose failures. Reports can contain an error and stack trace, app version, device model, operating-system version, available memory or disk information, time and a random installation identifier. Because Firebase Analytics is also included, a report may include recent Analytics event breadcrumbs showing actions immediately before the crash. In this game, those events can include case or product identifiers, counts and short status values; they do not include your typed detective name, free-form text or clue text. The game does not attach a custom user ID, custom keys or application log messages. Crash reports are sent independently of the analytics preference. Analytics breadcrumbs are included only when Analytics collection is enabled under the game's consent and settings.

Advertising and ad privacy

The game uses Google AdMob to serve ads. Season 1 and No Ads each remove interstitial ads. Optional rewarded videos remain available with or without a purchase, and AdMob serves them only when you choose to watch one. Interstitial ads may be offered after every three eligible completed cases (tutorial cases do not count), only when you continue from a result screen; a minimum interval may also apply. There is no startup delay or daily interstitial cap. Optional rewarded ads are shown only after you choose an offer, for example for an extra hint, an archive case or a streak repair. There are no banner ads.

AdMob may process IP address and approximate location, device and app information, advertising identifiers where available, and ad impressions or interactions to deliver, measure and protect ads. Google may use these data under its own privacy terms. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for ad consent before ad requests. Available choices depend on your region and the published message. If UMP provides a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen those choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices. On Android, you can also manage or reset the advertising ID in system settings. Current release testing covers Android; this notice will be updated before any iOS version is distributed.

Consent and privacy rights

Where the app asks for analytics consent, you can change or withdraw that choice in Settings → Privacy choices. Ad consent is handled separately by UMP. If a regional privacy message is available, its privacy-options entry point in Settings opens the choices relevant to your region; the options depend on the message and applicable rules. Depending on where you live, you may be able to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restrict or object to processing, or exercise other privacy rights. Contact us at wraithwick.studio@gmail.com. We will respond using the information you provide and may ask for a relevant app-instance identifier to locate analytics data.

Service providers and international processing

We use Google services for Firebase and AdMob, and the app stores for billing. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices and may process it in countries other than yours. The game has no Wraithwick-operated account or gameplay server.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the game, subject to your backup settings. Firebase and AdMob retain service data under their own settings and policies. You can turn analytics off in Settings → Privacy choices, change ad consent from Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option is available, and disable local reminders in Settings. For a request about personal data processed for the game, contact wraithwick.studio@gmail.com. Include the app-instance ID shown under Privacy choices if your request concerns analytics; do not send payment-card details. You may also complain to a privacy regulator where applicable.

Security

Information sent to Google services uses encrypted network connections. We do not operate a game account or progress server. You are responsible for protecting your device and any operating-system backup account.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children in breach of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal data to a service described here.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.
