Bureau of Unfinished Cases

English · Українська

A detective logic puzzle game for Android and iOS. Restless ghosts bring you their unsolved murders; you find the truth with pure logic, one clue at a time. Every case is fair: no guessing, ever.

Free to play with optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between cases. The one-time purchase Season 1 unlocks the current first-season content and full case archive, and removes ads between cases. Rewarded videos remain optional.
